Notation at a glance

Decode a move

Read each symbol in Qxf7+ from left to right.

Q x f7 +
  1. QPieceThe queen makes the move.
  2. xActionThe queen captures a piece.
  3. f7DestinationThe capture happens on square f7.
  4. +ResultThe move gives check.
03The Language of Chess

Modern chess uses algebraic notation. Every square has a name made from a letter for its file and a number for its rank. White starts from ranks 1 and 2, while Black starts from ranks 7 and 8.

Filesa through h, the vertical columns.
Ranks1 through 8, the horizontal rows.
SquaresA file and rank combine, such as e4.

Piece Symbols

Pieces use capital letters: K for king, Q for queen, R for rook, B for bishop, and N for knight. Pawns have no letter, so a pawn move uses only its destination square.

Nf3Knight moves to f3.

Captures and Checks

Use x when a piece captures. A check is marked with +, while checkmate is marked with #.

Qxf7+The queen captures on f7 and gives check.

Special Moves

  • O-O means kingside castling.
  • O-O-O means queenside castling.
  • e8=Q means a pawn promotes to a queen.
  • e.p. may describe an en passant capture.

How to Read a Move

In 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6, the number identifies the turn. White moves first, followed by Black. Read the sequence from left to right and use the coordinates to follow the position.

Practice tip: Write down five moves from your next game, then replay them later and check each square against the board.
