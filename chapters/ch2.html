Chapter 2 : The Pieces

Each piece has its own style and manner of moving in Chess. These different pieces are known as :-

  • Pawn
  • Rook
  • Knight
  • Bishop
  • Queen
  • King

Fun Fact

The square where the pieces are originally placed and is the default position of pieces is called home square.

The pawns

The Pawns, they move only in one direction, forward. But when capturing other pieces, they can also move diagonally! When they are on their home square, they can also move 2 steps.

Important Note : The Pawns are the only pieces that cannot move backward. While all the other pieces can move forward and backward.

The Rook

The Rook, is the most straightforward piece, it only moves vertically and horizontally, crushing whatever comes in its way! It could even capture a mighty Queen or measly Pawn. It was built to cause destruction on the board!

The Knight

The Knight is the most out of the ordinary piece on the board. It can move in an L-shaped way in any angle. It is frequently used in forks(A commonly used tactic that you will learn about later in CH-3.)

The Bishop

The Bishop is like a sniper, but on the board. It can move obliquely in any direction it wants. It is mostly kept in fianchetto (Another tactic that you will learn in CH-3.)

The Queen

The Queen is the most prevailing piece on the chess board. It can move diagonally and straightforward in any direction for any number of squares. You should avert getting out your queen too early.

The King

The King, the most mind-numbing piece and only has one move that is intriguing, i.e., THE CASTLING [A tactic that you will learn in CH-3.] It can move one square in any direction.
