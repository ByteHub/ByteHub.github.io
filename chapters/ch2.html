Chapter 2 : The Pieces

Each piece has its own style and manner of moving in Chess. These different pieces are known as:-

  • Pawn
  • Rook
  • Knight
  • Bishop
  • Queen
  • King

Fun Fact

The square where the pieces are originally placed and is the default position of pieces is called home square.

The pawns

  • The Pawns, they move only in one direction, forward. But when capturing other pieces, they can also move diagonally! When they are on their home square, they can also move 2 steps.

Important Note : The Pawns are the only pieces that cannot move backward. While all the other pieces can move forward and backward.
